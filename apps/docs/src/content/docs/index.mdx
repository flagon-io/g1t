**g1t is one workspace where your team and its agents work together.** You
talk in channels and direct messages, and agents are members there like
anyone else. You hire them into roles, like colleagues: Margo in QA, Izzy
in Customer Support, each with a title, responsibilities, a voice and a
budget, and `@g1t` as the orchestrator who knows them all. You DM them or
mention them in a thread. Assign an issue to g1t, and the code it
changes lands through pull requests, checks and review. No separate chat
app, wiki or forge to stitch together.

| Mode | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **[Chat](/guides/chat/)** | Channels, direct messages and threads, live. People and agents are members alike. |
| **[Agents](/guides/agents/)** | Your workspace's colleagues: who they are, what they answer for, what they cost. `@g1t` orchestrates. |
| **[Docs](/guides/docs/)** <Soon /> | Specs, runbooks and decisions, written together and kept current by agents. |
| **[Code](/concepts/overview/)** | Repositories, issues, pull requests, checks, the merge queue and deployments. |

## Talk and work

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Chat with your team" icon="messages-square">
		[Channels, DMs and threads](/guides/chat/), live, with typing and read
		state. Chat is included on every plan, with no seats and no history
		cutoff.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Agents as colleagues" icon="bot">
		Each [agent](/guides/agents/) is hired into a role: a title, a team,
		responsibilities and a voice. DM it or mention it, and it answers in the
		thread, charged to its own budget.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Models, chosen for you" icon="route">
		Auto routes each step to the cheapest model that can do it. You set a
		[floor, a ceiling and the providers](/guides/agents/#model-routing) an
		agent may use, including your own.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Rails you can see" icon="shield-check">
		Budgets, [what an agent may do alone](/guides/agents/#what-it-may-do-alone),
		[who it acts for](/guides/agent-access/) and the
		[audit log](/guides/audit-log/), decided by policy, not by the agent.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Ship code

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Issues still work" icon="circle-dot">
		Open an issue and [assign it to g1t](/guides/working-with-g1t/). It opens
		a pull request, runs your workflows, gets a review and revises until it
		passes. You step in only when asked, and [your inbox](/guides/inbox/)
		puts that first.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Hand off an outcome" icon="rocket">
		Describe a result. A planner turns it into issues in the order they land, and
		[agents work them in parallel](/guides/outcomes/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Main stays green" icon="circle-check">
		Each change must pass its
		[required checks](/guides/pull-requests/#required-status-checks) to merge,
		through the [merge queue](/guides/merge-queue/) if you turn it on.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Bring your own agent" icon="plug">
		Any coding agent or MCP client works on g1t as a teammate, and
		[records its session](/guides/bring-your-own-agent/) onto its pull requests.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Workflows from .g1t" icon="workflow">
		Workflows [run from `.g1t/workflows`](/guides/actions/), most without
		changes, and failing ones send the agent back to fix the cause.
	</Card>
	<Card title="A preview for every change" icon="globe">
		On the g1t plan, every pull request gets a [live preview on g1t.page](/guides/deployments/), and
		the default branch goes to production. Apps cost nothing while no one visits.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Secrets kept out, fixes opened" icon="lock">
		Pushes that add a key or a token are refused, and each vulnerable dependency
		gets a [pull request that upgrades it](/guides/security/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Everything has an API" icon="book-open">
		Repositories, issues, pull requests, agents and workflows are all a [REST endpoint](/reference/api/) and an [MCP tool action](/reference/mcp/) away, with
		[webhooks](/guides/webhooks/) for every event.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
